About DR NFH

Where Nutrition Meets
the Mind & Soul

Dr. Nida Fatima Hazari is a distinguished Nutritionist, Counselling Psychologist, Academic Researcher, and Global Health Advocate from Hyderabad, India. Her interdisciplinary work bridges nutrition science, psychology, and public health.

With a PhD in Foods & Nutrition from Telangana State Agricultural University, a PG Diploma in Counselling Psychology, and courses from the University of Oxford, she brings a truly holistic perspective to health and wellness.

As Founder & CEO of NFH Nutrition and Beyond LLP, she leads a mission to empower individuals, schools, and organisations to build healthier, more balanced lives through science, empathy, and education.
